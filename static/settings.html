Ad & Tracker Blocking

The proxy decodes every request a page makes, so ads and trackers are dropped before they reach the network. YouTube is handled differently: its ads travel inside the player response, from the same host as the video, so those get cut out of the response body instead — no pre-roll, no mid-roll, no sponsored results in search.
